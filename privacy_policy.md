# Privacy Policy for Smart Reserve Admin

**Last Updated:** February 8, 2026

## Introduction

Smart Reserve Admin ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when using the app:

- **Authentication Data:** Email address and password for account access (managed through Firebase Authentication)
- **Booking Information:** Name, token number, course code, booking dates, and time slot selections

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data:** App interaction data, features used, and timestamps

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

- ✅ Authenticate and manage admin user accounts
- ✅ Display and manage booking records
- ✅ Generate reports in PDF and Excel formats
- ✅ Provide booking management functionality
- ✅ Improve app performance and user experience

## Data Storage & Security

### Cloud Services
We utilize **Google Firebase** services:
- **Firebase Authentication:** Secure user authentication
- **Cloud Firestore:** Encrypted cloud database for booking data

### Local Storage
- Reports generated (PDF/Excel) are saved to your device's Documents folder
- Storage access is limited to app-generated files only

### Security Measures
- All data transmission is encrypted using industry-standard protocols
- Authentication tokens are securely managed
- We implement Firebase's security rules to protect data access

## Data Sharing

We **do not sell, trade, or rent** your personal information to third parties.

Data may be shared only:
- When required by law or legal process
- To protect our rights and safety
- With your explicit consent

## Data Retention

- **Account Data:** Retained while your account is active
- **Booking Records:** Retained as per organizational data retention policies
- **Local Reports:** Stored on your device until you delete them

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- 📋 **Access** your personal data
- ✏️ **Correct** inaccurate data
- 🗑️ **Delete** your account and associated data
- 📤 **Export** your booking records
- 🚫 **Withdraw** consent at any time

## Children's Privacy

This application is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
